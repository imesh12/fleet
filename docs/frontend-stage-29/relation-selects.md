# Relation Selects

The shared `RelationSelect` component loads active options from backend list endpoints and includes selected organization context.

Vehicle relations:

- customers: `/admin/customer-accounts`
- departments: `/admin/departments`
- business units: `/admin/business-units`
- vehicle types: `/admin/vehicle-types`
- vehicle groups: `/admin/vehicle-groups`
- makes: `/admin/vehicle-makes`
- models: `/admin/vehicle-models`

Driver relations:

- customers: `/admin/customer-accounts`
- departments: `/admin/departments`
- business units: `/admin/business-units`
- driver groups: `/admin/driver-groups`
